Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Plaxeron

How do I register on Plaxeron?
Click on the "Register" button in the navbar and choose either Student or Company registration. Fill in your details, and for students, you can start applying immediately. Companies need admin approval before posting drives.
Can I apply to multiple placement drives?
Yes! Students can apply to as many placement drives as they want. However, you can only apply once per drive. Your profile and resume are saved, making it easy to apply with just one click.
How long does company approval take?
The admin team typically reviews company registrations within 24 hours. You'll receive an email notification once your company profile is approved or if additional information is needed.
How do I track my application status?
After logging in to your student dashboard, go to "My Applications" to see real-time status updates for all your applications. You'll see whether you're Applied, Shortlisted, Placed, or Rejected for each drive.
What documents do I need to upload?
Students should upload their resume in PDF format. You may also be asked to add academic transcripts and certificates. Companies need to provide registration documents and company verification details during signup.
Is Plaxeron free to use?
Yes! Plaxeron is completely free to use for both companies and students.
