Frequently Asked Questions
How do I apply to a placement drive?
Go to the Placement Drives tab, find a drive you're eligible for, and click Apply Now. Ensure your profile and resume are complete before applying.
How do I update my profile?
Open the Edit Profile tab from the sidebar or profile menu to update your details. Changes are saved instantly.
Where can I see if I've been shortlisted or selected?
Check the Application History tab. Your latest status is always displayed there. You'll also receive a notification when your status changes.
Can I apply to multiple drives?
Yes! You can apply to as many open drives as you're eligible for. There is no limit, but check eligibility criteria for each drive.
Why can't I see certain drives?
Only drives approved by the admin are visible. New drives will appear once reviewed and approved by the placement cell.
